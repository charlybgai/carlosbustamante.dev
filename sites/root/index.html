About me

Cloud & MLOps Engineer

I'm a Cloud & MLOps Engineer based in Estado de México. I design and deploy the infrastructure that production AI runs on: AWS environments managed with Terraform, Kubernetes clusters on EKS, containerized model services, and LLM inference with vLLM.

I'm currently a Senior Software Engineer at GlobalLogic, working with Ring (an Amazon company) on AI agents that speed up internal product and engineering workflows. Before that, at Corteza.ai, I went from fine-tuning internal LLMs to architecting the AWS and Kubernetes platform behind the company's ML and LLM workloads. I hold a B.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering and an M.Sc. in Engineering Sciences from Tecnológico de Monterrey.
